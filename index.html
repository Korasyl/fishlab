ARCA FISH LAB

Shape a fish, paint it, watch it swim, save it for the aquarium.

Body

Tap any part of the fish to select it. Drag a handle to reshape. Double-tap the highlighted line to add a handle; double-tap a handle to remove it.

Fins and tail

Tap a fin to select it. Drag the fin itself to slide it along the body, or across to the other edge. Drag its square handles to reshape it; double-tap its outline to add a handle, double-tap a handle to remove it.

Getting it into the game

  1. Save the file.
